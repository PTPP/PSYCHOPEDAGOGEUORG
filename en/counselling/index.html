Psychopedagogical Association · Bolesławiec

Individual psychosocial counselling

A practical place to discuss stress, emotions, relationships and everyday challenges. Begin with a single consultation and consider further sessions if they are useful.

What can we talk about?

Topics may include stress, personal relationships, family difficulties, overwhelming responsibilities, decision-making and emotional wellbeing. We discuss what you would like to change without promising a guaranteed outcome.

Initial and follow-up consultations

A standard appointment lasts 50 minutes and costs 200 PLN. There is no obligation to purchase a fixed package. Where a structured programme is appropriate, it is discussed separately.

In person or online

Consultations are available in Bolesławiec or online, depending on the service and appointment availability.

What counselling does not cover

Our services are psychosocial and educational rather than medical. They do not replace psychiatric assessment, medical treatment or medical psychotherapy.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book multiple sessions?

No. You can start with a single consultation.

How long is a session?

The standard individual consultation lasts 50 minutes.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes, an online counselling session is included in our service list.

These services are educational and psychosocial and do not replace medical care.
