01

Psychological Assessment for ADHD

A three-stage process for children, adolescents and adults: an independent initial consultation (600 PLN) which may conclude the service, further assessment (600 PLN) after acceptance of a diagnostic service agreement, and a written report (300 PLN). An additional in-depth results discussion is optional and costs 300 PLN. Each stage is paid for separately. Psychiatric consultation is provided by a separate medical provider and is not included. Any additional assessments and fees are agreed in advance.

1500 PLN3-stage process

25

Developmental Insight (Ages 5–16)

An innovative, multi-stage child development assessment program comprising 5 individual diagnostic-observational sessions, culminating in a detailed, comprehensive report. A holistic view of your child's potential and needs — no waiting lists, in a comfortable setting. Please note: this assessment is supportive and developmental in nature and does not replace a formal psychological-pedagogical evaluation or medical diagnosis.

1800 PLN5 Sessions + Document
