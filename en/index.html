06 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What to expect when arranging your first appointment.

Who is online counselling (online sessions) for?

Online counselling (often colloquially referred to as "online therapy") is for individuals who want to work on stress, relationships, emotions, and daily functioning, while needing flexibility: they live abroad, have difficulty commuting, or prefer contact from home. At the start, it is worth ensuring a stable internet connection and a quiet place to talk.

What is the difference between a psychologist and a counsellor (certified advisor)?

A psychologist is a person with a master's degree in psychology and credentials to practice the profession. A counsellor / certified psychosocial advisor can have different baseline education (e.g. psychology, pedagogy), and their readiness for work is confirmed by a certification process. In practice: a psychologist more often focuses on clinical diagnosis, while a counsellor focuses on direct support, skills training, psychoeducation, and daily coping with difficulties.

What is the difference between psychotherapy and counseling / psychosocial support?

Psychotherapy is a specialized form of work aimed at clinical treatment and mental disorders. Counseling and psychosocial support (counselling) focuses on building resources, psychoeducation, and practical tools for coping with situational difficulties and stress. Our model does not replace medical treatment – if we see such a need, we help choose a diagnostic and medical path.

Do you provide diagnoses?

We offer a three-stage psychological assessment for ADHD (1500 PLN: 600 + 600 + 300 PLN), including an interview, psychological testing and a report with feedback. This does not replace a separate psychiatric consultation and medical assessment provided outside the Association.

Do you treat illnesses?

We do not conduct treatment of illnesses or medical therapy. Our actions are supporting, interventionist, and developmental: we provide counselling, psychoeducation, crisis intervention, and skills training for coping with emotions.

Do you issue certificates and opinions for courts / schools?

Yes. We distinguish two types of documents: (1) Certificates – shorter confirmations of participation, scope of work, and observations. (2) Opinions of the Psychopedagogical Association – detailed, multi-page developments based on implemented tools and observations. Opinions are private opinions and can serve as auxiliary material; they do not replace an opinion of a court-appointed expert.

Do you execute court orders regarding "therapy" or competence improvement?

We review the court order and the services we can lawfully provide. Private reports do not replace official court expert opinions; the recipient decides their evidential value.

How to prepare for the first meeting?

You do not need to prepare in any special way. It is enough if you come with readiness to talk and a basic definition of what you are currently dealing with and what you would like to change in your life. The rest of the meeting will be guided by our specialist.
