Certificates and private reports

An attendance certificate and a detailed private assessment report are different types of documents, with different purposes and fees.

Attendance certificate — 300 PLN

The published service description specifies a minimum of two sessions and preparation within seven working days. A certificate can confirm participation and the appropriate limited scope of services.

Private psychopedagogical report — from 5,000 PLN

A more extensive private report requires separate assessment work and agreed terms. The final fee depends on the scope.

Use in formal proceedings

Private documents are not the same as an opinion issued by a court-appointed expert. The receiving court or authority determines their evidential value.

Privacy and consent

Sharing information about an individual with an external recipient requires an appropriate legal basis and verification of the intended recipient.

Frequently asked questions

Are attendance certificates free?

No. The listed standard fee is 300 PLN.

Does every detailed report cost 5,000 PLN?

No. That is the starting price and the scope is quoted individually.

Does a private report replace a court expert opinion?

No. These are legally different documents.

These services are educational and psychosocial and do not replace medical care.
