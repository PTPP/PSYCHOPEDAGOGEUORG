ADHD psychological assessment

A structured three-stage assessment process for children, adolescents and adults. The psychological report can inform a separate medical consultation; it does not replace a psychiatrist's assessment.

Who is this for?

The process is intended for people experiencing persistent difficulties with attention, activity or impulsivity which may require further assessment. The goal is to evaluate the available information, not to promise a particular diagnosis.

The three assessment stages

  1. Initial consultation — 600 PLN. An interview, observation and preliminary review of difficulties, developmental history and available records. Documents may also be submitted afterwards for review within this stage. The client receives verbal preliminary findings and recommendations and may stop after this consultation without committing to further assessment.
  2. Psychological assessment — 600 PLN. Before proceeding, the client accepts a diagnostic service agreement covering the scope and terms. The psychologist selects methods appropriate to the person's age and circumstances, including interviews, observation and information from relevant settings. Written observations from family members, schools or others may be used where appropriate. The number of appointments depends on diagnostic needs and the agreed scope.
  3. Written assessment report — 300 PLN. Findings are integrated and summarised in a written report with conclusions and recommendations. Basic feedback about the assessment outcome is part of the assessment process. An additional in-depth appointment to discuss the results and recommendations is optional and costs 300 PLN.

After receiving the report

You can take the report to an independent psychiatrist. A medical assessment and any treatment decisions are separate from the psychological assessment and are not included in the 1,500 PLN fee.

Additional assessment needs

Sometimes additional tests are useful. If that happens, we'll explain which tests we recommend, why, and how much they cost. We'll only carry them out with your agreement. Previous school reports or assessments can be useful if available.

Frequently asked questions

Does the report equal a medical diagnosis?

No. It is a psychological assessment report. Medical assessment is provided separately.

Is payment made in three stages?

Yes. The first consultation costs 600 PLN and may be the only stage you choose. If you continue, the diagnostic service agreement is accepted before stage two. The full assessment with a report costs 1,500 PLN (600 + 600 + 300 PLN). An optional additional detailed feedback appointment costs 300 PLN.

Can children be assessed?

Yes. The service covers children, adolescents and adults.

These services are educational and psychosocial and do not replace medical care.
