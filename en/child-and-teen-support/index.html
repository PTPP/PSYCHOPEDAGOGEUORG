Psychopedagogical Association · Bolesławiec

Support for children and teenagers

Psychopedagogical support focused on emotions, school-related difficulties, peer relationships and everyday coping skills.

Areas of support

An individual session can address emotional regulation, school demands, peer interactions, communication and related developmental needs.

Individual appointments

A standard 50-minute support session costs 200 PLN. Activities are adapted to the child's age and assessed needs.

Social skills training

The published offer includes group-based social skills training (TUS) at 110 PLN per 60-minute session. Group availability should be confirmed before enrolment.

Working with parents

The role of parents or guardians is determined according to the child's age, needs, privacy and the applicable rules for representation of minors. This support does not replace medical assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Does a parent attend every session?

It depends on the young person's age, needs and the arrangements with the practitioner.

Is TUS a group service?

Yes. The published TUS service is group-based.

Does an appointment provide a medical diagnosis?

No. It is psychopedagogical support rather than medical diagnosis.

These services are educational and psychosocial and do not replace medical care.
