Parenting skills course

A ten-hour educational programme for parents and caregivers who want to develop communication, emotional regulation and practical parenting skills.

Group and individual options

  • Group programme: 10 hours, 1,500 PLN.
  • Individual programme: 10 hours with a practitioner, 3,500 PLN.
Both versions are listed as including a final exam and certificate under the course terms.

When might the programme be useful?

The programme may support personal development or be considered when a court order refers to parenting skills. In the latter case, the actual order and the organisation's scope must be reviewed first.

What does the certificate mean?

A course certificate does not guarantee acceptance by a court or other authority. The receiving authority decides whether and how the document is used.

Before enrolling

Indicate whether the course is for personal development or to address an existing formal requirement. This allows staff to assess the appropriate next step.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours are included?

Ten hours for either format.

Will I receive a certificate?

The published course description includes a final exam and certificate.

Will a court accept it?

Acceptance cannot be guaranteed and is determined by the receiving authority.

These services are educational and psychosocial and do not replace medical care.
